A token lets an app on your computer, such as the SAR Workbench MCP server in Kiro, act as you. It can see exactly the projects you can see, and its activity is recorded under your name. Anyone who has the token can use it, so keep it private and revoke it if it leaks. Tokens stop working if your portal access to SAR Workbench is removed.
