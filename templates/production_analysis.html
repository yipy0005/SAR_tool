Look for patterns in your results

Why in chemistryStructure–activity reasoning is strongest when each pattern is reproducible, linked to compatible measurements, and treated as a question for review rather than a prediction.

Build a model only from compatible observed results

The workbench uses a deterministic RDKit similarity model with leave-one-out or project-holdout checks, a declared applicability threshold, and persisted uncertainty. It cannot issue predictions from demo data, censored values, missing results, or a model that fails its validation gate.

Why in chemistryInternal cross-validation is evidence about this project and endpoint—not proof that the model generalizes to a new chemical series or laboratory.
